The bigger picture

The committee's rule of thumb in every debrief: skills and habits can be coached; ethics, safety, and beliefs about students are much harder to change. Time management, parent communication, and stress regulation respond to mentoring and a supportive school. A failure to report suspected abuse, an inappropriate relationship with students, dishonesty, or a fixed belief that some students can't learn put children at risk on day one. Research backs up both halves. Coaching moves practice, and schools with strong professional environments grow their teachers faster. Still, no single interview signal predicts success, so good principals read the whole file.
